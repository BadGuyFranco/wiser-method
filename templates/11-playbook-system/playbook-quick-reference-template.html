Chapter 11 . Playbook System

Playbook Structure

Section What It Captures When
Metadata Project name, team, dates, Playbook Keeper (the Architect) Day one
Team Structure Position assignments; who owns which tensions Day one
Current State What you observed during Witness; the baseline Day one
Open Questions Hypotheses to test in Interrogate; unknowns Day one
Objectives What success looks like; constraints on the solution space After Witness
Risk Register What could derail the project; impact and mitigation Day one
Decision Log Every significant decision with rationale and alternatives First decision
Hierarchy of Agency AI autonomous vs. human approval vs. human-only decisions Solve
Owner

The Architect. Always. One person, clear accountability. On day one, your Playbook is mostly questions. By the end, it's mostly answers.

Maintenance Rules

  • Update same-day, not "when we have time"
  • Every experiment result gets recorded
  • Every decision includes rationale
  • Version the Playbook for after-action reviews
When It Becomes Bureaucracy

If updates feel like homework, strip back to decisions and learnings only. Ask: what do we actually reference? Keep that. Archive everything else.

Warning Signs

Symptom Problem
Decisions get relitigatedRationale not documented
Describes a different system than what's runningUpdates deferred
New members can't onboard from itToo fragmented or incomplete
Risk conversations start from scratchRisk register not maintained
Nobody opens it between meetingsCompliance artifact, not working doc

Playbook by Canon

Canon What Gets Added
WitnessCurrent state, friction maps, user flows
InterrogateAssumptions, experiment results, validated/invalidated
SolveSolution design, pilot results, quality framework
ExpandContext assessments, deployment gates, scaling decisions
RefineHierarchy of agency, drift findings, incident responses

Day-One Checklist

  • Project name and dates filled in
  • Position assignments documented
  • Current state captured (even 2-3 sentences)
  • Initial open questions listed
  • Known risks identified
  • Playbook Keeper (Architect) assigned
The One-Minute Test

When someone asks "why did we do it this way?", can you answer from the Playbook in under a minute? If not, something is missing.
